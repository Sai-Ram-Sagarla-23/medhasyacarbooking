How it works

Rental terms

Valid driving licence and a government photo ID required. Advance is adjusted in the final bill. Refundable security deposit is collected at pickup. Fuel is return-as-received. Extra km beyond the daily limit and late returns are charged extra. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup.
